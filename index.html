SetAnvil Support

Need help? Open a support request and we'll get back to you within two business days. Requests are public, so please don't include personal details like your email or password.

How do I import my plan from Notes?

Plans → Import from Notes, then paste. Use a header like “Day 1 Push”, the exercise name, then one line per set: weight, a tab, reps (e.g. “185⇥8”). “BW” means bodyweight. One-liners like “Squat 4x6 185” work too.

How does the rest timer work?

Tap Start set, then Done. The rest timer starts automatically using that exercise's rest time. You'll get a vibration and a notification when rest is over, and another if you rest 30 seconds too long.

How do I add weight?

Tap + on an exercise during a workout, or use “+ all” in the plan editor. After a workout, SetAnvil suggests a bump for every lift where you hit all your targets.

Workouts aren't showing in Apple Health

Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → SetAnvil and allow Workouts. Also check SetAnvil → Settings → Save workouts to Apple Health.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account & data.
