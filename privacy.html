SetAnvil Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

Your workouts stay on your iPhone. We only store what's needed to sign you in. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Plans, sets, weights, workout history, meals, and water intake are saved in SetAnvil's private storage on your iPhone, encrypted by iOS Data Protection. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Face ID / Touch ID, if you turn it on, is handled entirely by iOS. SetAnvil never sees your biometric data.

Account information

If you create an account, your name, email address, and a securely hashed password are stored by our authentication provider, Supabase, solely to sign you in, confirm your email, and reset your password. You can use SetAnvil without an account.

Apple Health

If you allow it, SetAnvil:

  • Writes each finished workout (as a strength-training workout) and the water you log.
  • Reads heart rate recorded by your Apple Watch, to show it live during a workout and save your average and max with that workout in the app.
  • Reads workouts around the time of your session, only to avoid saving a duplicate if your Apple Watch already recorded the same workout.

Health data stays on your iPhone. It is never sent to our servers, used for advertising or marketing, sold, or shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps.

Notifications

Rest-timer alerts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No notification data leaves your device.

Deleting your data

In SetAnvil, go to Settings → Delete account & data. This permanently deletes your account from our servers and erases your workout data from your iPhone. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Children

SetAnvil is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Open a support request. Requests are public, so please don't include personal details.
